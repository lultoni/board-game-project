# Open Playtest Ideas

This doc is designed to list all ideas that currently could be used inside an upcoming playtest.
Every Idea has a name, description but most importantly a problem is trying to solve and an activation trigger.

Activation trigger does not mean it will be played next - just that it will be considered in the next rule   chaning brainstorm.

## List of Ideas

- Name: Dual-Counter Combo
    - Problem it solves:
        - Adding an attacker-counter alongside the target-counter rewards distributing pressure across multiple fronts, dissolving the exchange-pit pattern.
        - Late game offensive lockout (only 1 champ with strike left) is lowered or removed
        - Mid-game exchange pit removed
    - Concrete Rule Change:
        - When the same friendly Champion hits two different opponents pieces (with skills only), they get bonus damage for hitting the second piece
    - Risks I see:
        - Standoff increases due to no daring to enter radius of a single opponents champ
        - Allows single champ to wipe out a lot of pieces just for moving forward
- Name: King Lifetime HP
    - Problem it solves:
        - King (or any piece perchance) can survive indefinitly without being able to be killed (via healing or armor stacking)
    - Concrete Rule Change:
        - Each King can only take x Damage over the entire game before being killed no matter the current health
    - Risks I see:
        - Ultra defensive king play is encouraged with never moving the king out or always just running it into the corner furthest away from opponents pieces
        - At a certain point it will no longer make sense to actually heal your king or give it armor since lifetime hp are lower anyways
- Name: Armor Decay
    - Problem it solves:
        - Players are stacking up too much armor without being active in the game
    - Concrete Rule Change:
        - Armor gets universally ticked down at a certain rate
    - Risks I see:
        - Armor will become unusable since you just lose your money you put in for nothing
        - Does not attack the core problem of why armor is being used too much
- Name: piece count reduction
    - Problem it sovlves:
        - increases per piece importance
        - lowers decision density (branching)
        - shortens game length directly
    - Concrete Rule change:
    - Risk I see:
        - leaves less room to actually setup complex/interesting situations
        - reduces combo diversity
        - forces players to nail down certain playstyles which can lead to being fully countered by the opposing player
