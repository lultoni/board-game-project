### Section A: General Impressions

**Favourite moment**: "Als mit Air Blast ein Ziel gerade so weggeworfen wurde und dadurch sein Positioning off war und er nicht angreifen konnte." *(When Air Blast pushed a target just barely out of position so their positioning was off and they couldn't attack.)*

**Most confusing/frustrating rule**: "Manche Regeln / Skill Funktionalitäten waren nicht ganz klar im Cheat Sheet und den Skill Cards." *(Some rules / skill functionalities weren't entirely clear on the Cheat Sheet and the Skill Cards.)*

**Game length (1-5)**: **4** (slightly too long). "Das Endgame hat sich über 10+ Runden gezogen, obwohl quasi schon klar war, dass ich gewinnen würde (ohne Selbstfehler at least)." *(The endgame dragged on for 10+ rounds even though it was basically already clear I would win (barring self-error at least).)*

**Skill Drafting**: "War balanced, die Skills die zur Auswahl stehen, entscheiden halt nur viel über den Verlauf dann auch." *(Was balanced, but the skills available for selection end up deciding a lot about how the game goes.)*

**Piece Placement**: "Yopp, man hat am Anfang direkt bis nur Shield gemacht und erste Kontakte (mid-game) war nach ~7 Runden." *(Yep, at the start you'd just shield up directly and first contacts (mid-game) were after ~7 rounds.)*

**Rune Gain pace**: "Am Anfang hatte man halt wenig und am Ende hat man nur darauf gewartet, bis man genug hatte." *(At the start you had little and at the end you were just waiting until you had enough.)*

**Skill Slots enough?**: "Ja, die Runes waren der limiting factor." *(Yes, the Runes were the limiting factor.)*

**Move Slots + Skill Slots intuitive?**: "Ja." *(Yes.)*

**Bodyguard Rule**: "Kam nicht ins Spiel, Guards waren schnell weg." *(Didn't come into play, Guards were gone quickly.)*

**Who should choose which Guard is sacrificed?**: *(not answered)*

**Injured effect (Speed=1, Range-1) fair?**: "Sehr valide (Range hatte man aber eher selten gehabt, too), kam nur selten dazu." *(Very valid (Range was rarely relevant though, too), rarely came up.)*

**Armor cap of 3**: "Mit Rust Shield kann man halt quasi mehr Armor als das erlaubt waren — 0 (Ranges waren bis man dann wichtig der Wert, weil man sonst könnte will 1 piece 5 HP hätte." *(With Rust Shield you can effectively get more armor than... Ranges were important because otherwise you could have a piece with 5 HP.)* — Note: somewhat hard to parse, but the gist is that with Rust Shield (self +1 Armor) a piece can effectively reach very high survivability, which felt concerning.

**Skill Path blockage**: "Kam so kaum nicht ins Spiel, aber war eigentlich okay so." *(Barely came into play, but was actually okay that way.)*

**Over/under-powered skills**: "Ich glaube man müsste halt manche Sachen spezifizieren, aber sonst okay. Man hat sich sehr auf den günstigste Skills fokussiert — Field Medic, (Armor Smiths) rarely used. Momentan noch weniger taktisch (command statt das damage?). Blade Call insta Burst müsste ngl." *(I think some things need to be specified more, but otherwise okay. Players heavily focused on the cheapest skills — Field Medic, Armorsmith rarely used. Currently less tactical. Blade Call insta-burst was notable.)*

### Section C: Final Thoughts

**(Do you think the starting player has an inherent advantage?)**: "War sehr balanced so, es hatte niemand per se so eine krasse suggestive Advantage. Ich glaube das Game feel war sehr stark so 'warte bis man voll eine Figur nehmen kann' und dann halt sehr gezogen." *(Was quite balanced, nobody had an inherent obvious advantage. I think the game feel was very strongly 'wait until you can fully take a piece' and then very drawn out.)*

## Pasco (Feedback)

### Section A: General Impressions

**Favourite moment**: "Using Shadow Shift skill." *(Shadow Shift / Schattenwechsel — swap positions with ally.)*

**Most confusing/frustrating rule**: "Every piece can jump on any piece and makes the same damage points." *(Unclear that all standard attacks deal the same 2 DMG regardless of piece type.)*

**Game length (1-5)**: **4** (slightly too long).

### Section B: Systems Feedback

**Board size (10x10)**: "In general good size at some points (beginning) too much empty space." *(Generally good size but too much empty space at the start.)*

**Skill Drafting**: "It was fair system. In the end a little slow to get started — many rounds no skill use (too less points)." *(Fair system but slow start — many rounds without skill use due to insufficient Runes.)*

**Piece Placement**: "It was good balanced."

**Rune Gain pace**: "Too Slow — at first it starts very slow with many rounds of not much gain, so it was not usable skill there. Maybe start +2 gain." *(Too slow at the start. Suggested starting at +2 Rune gain.)*

**Skill Slots enough?**: "Yes."

**Move Slots + Skill Slots intuitive?**: "Maybe not as much intuitive as not expecting it, but the good part was you were able to use skills from other pieces than the moved ones." *(Not immediately intuitive, but appreciated that unlinked movement/action meant any piece could use skills.)*

**Bodyguard Rule**: "More of a minor obstacle, there was no game moment where it was used." *(Minor obstacle, never triggered.)*

**Who should choose which Guard is sacrificed?**: "Defender — your own pieces should be managed by yourself."

**Injured effect (Speed=1, Range-1) fair?**: "There was not a real use of it, closing the play — good idea maybe more penalizing (ex. 3HP/Piece, 2HP -> penalty)." *(Not really used in practice. Suggested maybe 3HP per piece with Injured at 2HP.)*

**Armor cap of 3**: "It's fine, you can make a good amount of extra hit points, but in the end you can still hit all 5 HP at once." *(Fine as-is — you can stack up to effective 5HP but a standard attack still deals 2 DMG.)*

**Skill Path blockage**: "Skill path should be blocked by all pieces." *(Agree with current rules.)*

**Over/under-powered skills**: "Shadow Shift is O.P. in current rules — you change place all over the map." *(Shadow Shift is overpowered — lets you swap position anywhere on the map.)*

### Section C: Final Thoughts

**(Do you think the starting player has an inherent advantage?)**: *(No strong opinion expressed.)*

**Suggestion**: "Maybe try 3 movements in movement phase." *(Increase Move Slots from 2 to 3.)*

### Key Data Points from Game Logs

- **Game lasted ~28-35 rounds** — very long for a tactical game
- **First capture around Round 7** (Elias's log)
- **Rune spending pattern**: Both players consistently spent down to 0-2 Runes, suggesting Runes were always the bottleneck (never had "too many")
- **Early rounds (1-6)**: Very low Rune pools (1-3), minimal skill use — confirms "slow start" feedback
- **Mid-game (7-14)**: Rune gain scaling kicks in, more active turns
- **Late game (15+)**: Runes climbing into 4-8 range but still fully spent each round — escalation working but game not ending

## Synthesis: Key Findings

### Confirmed Design Strengths
1. **10x10 board size works** — both players said size was generally right. Not too big overall, though early game still has too much empty space.
2. **Unlinked Movement/Action appreciated** — Pasco specifically liked being able to use skills from non-moved pieces. Elias found it intuitive.
3. **Skill Drafting feels fair** — but Elias noted it determines a lot of the game outcome.
4. **Piece Placement balanced** — no complaints.
5. **Skill Slots were sufficient** — Runes, not Slots, were the limiting factor.
6. **Move Slots + Skill Slots separation is intuitive** (Elias: "Ja").

### Confirmed Design Problems

1. **GAME IS TOO LONG** — Both rated 4/5 (too long). ~28-35 rounds. Endgame dragged on 10+ rounds even when outcome was "decided." This is the #1 problem.

2. **RUNE ECONOMY IS TOO SLOW AT THE START** — Both players felt starved of Runes early. Pasco: "many rounds no skill use." Pasco suggested starting at +2 gain. First ~6 rounds are mostly just positioning with no skill interaction — skills are what make this game unique, so those rounds are the game at its least interesting.

3. **BODYGUARD RULE NEVER TRIGGERED** — Both players reported it didn't come into play. Elias: "Guards waren schnell weg." Guards were eliminated before they could bodyguard. This means either:
   - Guards die too easily (standard attack = instant kill)
   - Players don't position Guards defensively
   - The adjacency-to-both requirement is too restrictive

4. **SHADOW SHIFT (SWAP) IS OVERPOWERED** — Pasco flagged it explicitly. No range limit on the swap makes it a dominant repositioning tool. Should have a range restriction.

5. **INJURED STATE RARELY RELEVANT** — Both said it barely came up. Standard attacks deal 2 DMG (instant kill on Normal pieces), so pieces skip Injured and go straight to dead. The Injured state only matters if skills (1 DMG) are used offensively, but players focused on cheap/efficient plays. The 2HP system may need rethinking.

6. **DEFENSIVE SKILLS UNDERUSED** — Elias noted Field Medic and Armorsmith were "rarely used." Players gravitated toward cheap offensive/utility skills. The healing/armor economy doesn't seem to offer enough return on investment compared to just attacking.

7. **BLADE CALL BURST FEELS STRONG** — Elias flagged Blade Call (pay +1 Rune for +1 DMG on next skill) as enabling strong burst combos. Combined with a Strike skill, it can deal 2 DMG from range — effectively a ranged instant kill.

8. **"WAIT AND POUNCE" FEEL** — Elias described the game feel as "wait until you can fully take a piece, then it's very drawn out." This suggests the game lacks moment-to-moment tension in the mid-game — players save up and then go all-in rather than making interesting incremental trades.

### New Design Hypotheses

1. **Start Rune gain at 2 instead of 1** — directly addresses the slow start.
2. **Add a range limit to Shadow Shift** — e.g., Range 2 or Skill Path required.
3. **3 Move Slots instead of 2** — Pasco's suggestion. Would increase tempo, reduce "dead" positioning turns.
4. **Consider 3HP per piece** (Pasco's idea) — would make Injured state actually relevant and create more incremental damage opportunities.
5. **Bodyguard adjacency requirement may need loosening** — if it never triggers, it might as well not exist. Test "adjacent to defender only" variant.
6. **Endgame acceleration mechanic needed** — the game needs a way to force a conclusion once one player has a clear advantage. The current draw condition (10 rounds no capture) is too generous.
