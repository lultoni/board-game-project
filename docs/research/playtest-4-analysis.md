## Session 22 amendment (2026-05-29 designer pass)

The body below is the original synthesis. After the cascade landed, the designer reviewed every finding and pushed back. The corrections below override the original framings on these specific points — read them first.

- **Combo bonus Q3 reward-feel softness is design-aligned, NOT a problem.** The bonus is by design a few-times-a-game payoff, not "do or lose." Boosting strength would push it toward "do or lose." The lever is **scope, not strength**. The original framing as "weak in feel" was wrong.
- **Finding #6 (Elias's late-game Blade Tempest spam) reframed.** Original read assumed he chose single-Champ bursts over the bonus loop. Actually his Strike-equipped Champions were dead. *"I did not have any other attack champs left."* This is a **structural offensive lockout**, not a behavioural choice. Justifies the attacker-counter half of the dual-counter design.
- **Finding #3 (must-pick density) softer than written.** Focus is equipped 1/2 across the army, not per-Champion. Armor 2/3 across the army. Catalogue pressure is per-loadout, not per-piece.
- **Finding #2 (Niko's 22 Armor vs Elias's 14)** has additional cause: Elias's Armor Breaker Champion died early before it could get value, so he had less Armor-stripping pressure. Asymmetry is partly stack-failure, not pure "first-time player Armor-stacked more."
- **Q-D1 teacher-vocab-checklist DEFERRED**, not made Priority 2. Lower-priority lever: skill names were shortened or replaced with natural words at the table — vocabulary barrier could be reduced by **renaming**, not stricter teaching. Bundled with Phase B naming pass.
- **"Don't know what to do" pattern decomposed** into two distinct windows: (a) opening positioning — no Strike skills firing in opening, only Defense (OQ-59a); (b) post-mid-game-exchange endgame conversion gap (OQ-59b). Two sub-problems.
- **Mid-game exchange-pit pattern** (Elias's "once an exchange starts, all action happens there until end") elevated to its own structural diagnosis — OQ-58. Attacker-counter half of dual-counter is the targeted antidote (rewards distributing pressure across multiple fronts).
- **Combo scope widening designed**: dual-counter (target + attacker) replacing simple single-counter scope expansion. Standard Attacks excluded (free, would over-cheapen the trigger). Multi-target skills tick all hits (watch flag for OP rollback). Both counters stack if a hit qualifies for both. Staged in `docs/backpocket.md` as Stack A G3, gated behind Stack H per Path A methodology decision.

Three new OQs opened (OQ-58 exchange-pit, OQ-59 opening+endgame don't-know-what-to-do, OQ-60 cognitive load watch). Six new backpocket entries (Combo Bonus Dual-Counter, Plague, Lucky/Star Strike, Focus replacement, Lance/Rune-Theft merge, Runes rename). See `game-state/SESSION_LOG.md` Session 22 for full discussion narrative.
