Rules Cheat Sheet: (GAME NAME)

#### **Objective**

- Capture the opponent's King.

**Draw Conditions:**

- No piece is captured for 10 full rounds.
- Only the two Kings remain on the board.

#### **Turn Structure**

- A Round = Player 1's Turn, then Player 2's Turn. A Player's Turn = Movement Phase, then Action Phase.

**1\. Movement Phase (2 optional Move Slots)**

- Use a Move Slot to move one piece.
- Base Speed:
  - Guard: 2 tiles
  - Champion/King: 1 tile
- Movement is in any direction (horizontal, vertical, diagonal).
- Injured pieces have a maximum speed of 1 (This is the minimum speed).

**2\. Action Phase (Starts with 2 optional Skill Slots)**

- Use a Skill Slot to activate an equipped Skill on a Champion or King.
- You must pay the Skill's Rune cost.

#### **Core Mechanics**

**Health & Damage**

- All pieces have 2 Health Points (HP).
- Normal State: Full health.
- Injured State: 1 HP remaining.
  - Effect: Movement is reduced to max 1 tile. Skill Range is reduced by 1.
  - Can be healed by Skill.
- Standard Attack (2 DMG): Move onto an opponent's tile. This instantly removes a Normal piece. Your piece takes its place.  
   If the piece is not removed (e.g. Armor) your piece does not move to the target tile (Speed 2: move to adjacent tile).
- Skills (Typically 1 DMG): Changes a Normal piece to Injured, or removes an Injured piece.

**Armor**

- Each point of Armor absorbs 1 DMG from a single source, then is destroyed.
- A piece can have a maximum of 3 Armor points.
- Does not negate Injured Effect.

**Bodyguard Rule**

- To attack a King or Champion, the target cannot have a friendly Guard adjacent to both the attacker's starting tile and the defender's tile.
- If a valid Guard is present, the attack is intercepted. The attacker does not move to the target square (Speed 2: move to adjacent tile), and the defending player chooses one of the valid Guards to be removed from the game instead.

#### **Resources & Progression**

Runes (R)

- Gain: +1 R at the end of each Round.
- Scaling: Gain increases by +1 R every 7 rounds (i.e., +2 at Round 7, +3 at Round 14, etc.).

**Skill Slots**

- Start: 2 Skill Slots.
- Scaling: Gain +1 Skill Slot every 10 rounds (i.e., 3 at Round 10, 4 at Round 20, etc.).  
   <br/>

#### **Skill System**

#### Skill Path: A straight line (horizontal, vertical, or diagonal) like a chess Queen. Blocked by opponents' pieces

- Skill Cost has to be paid before the Skill Effect takes Place
- Skill Effect, Cost, Name and Icon are on the Skill Cards
