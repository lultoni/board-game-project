A long time ago there was nothing except the primordial, wandering
through the barren void. Everything was nothing and nothing was
everything. Tranquility and peace marked this age, this was the norm, as
the primordial had no need for amusement, balance of all forces being
the main goal.

One of the many beings wandering endlessly was feeling different for
some reason. This being was commonly referred to as "Rabbit" by the
others. "Rabbit" did not like this emptiness, what was existence when
you did nothing but exist without purpose?

Over the next millennium he spread his beliefs around reaching many,
many surprised, many confused, many angry, many supportive. They decided
to create beings autonomous from them, was it entertainment or just
exploiting power will never be known.

The universe was to be altered indefinitely, one thing was clear
however, who had made the best creation?

A game was needed to be made to pitch challengers against each other.

brute strength, rather on strategy.
