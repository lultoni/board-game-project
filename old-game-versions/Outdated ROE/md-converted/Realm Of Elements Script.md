When your movement phase is done, you progress to the attack phase, the
last one before the opponent's one. Here you can cast spells until you
reach your spell limit and you can also attack one of your opponents
pieces directly in what I call a 1v1.

If you attack a guard with a mage both pieces will be taken from the

board is picked and removed. There is a special rule to avoid this, like
