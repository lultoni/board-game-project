Title: \"Realm of Elements\"

Overview:

\"Realm of Elements\" is a two-player perfect information strategy board
game that takes place in a mystical world where players compete as
powerful elemental mages seeking to harness the forces of nature and
magic to gain control over the realm. The game combines elements of
strategy, tactics, and spellcasting.

Components:

1\. Game Board: The game board consists of a square grid with 8x8 cells.
Each cell represents a specific terrain type, such as Forest, Mountain,
Lake, and Plains.

2\. Elemental Mages: Each player has a set of 5 elemental mage pieces,
representing Fire, Water, Earth, Air, and a Spirit Mage. These pieces
are uniquely shaped and colored.

3\. Guards: Each player also has a set of 5 guard pieces, which play a
protective role in the game.

4\. Spells: There is a table of spells that players can cast during
their turns. These spells are categorized into offensive, defensive, and
utility spells.

Setup:

1.  Place the game board in the center of the table.

2.  Each player chooses a color and places their elemental mages and
    guard pieces on their side of the board. The setup is as follows:\

3.  Spells: Every element has three spells, which can be cast using
    spell tokens. Both players start with 5 and get 1 more with every
    turn (except the starting player), and every 5 turns the addtion
    increases by 1.

Gameplay:

\"Realm of Elements\" is a turn-based game, and players take alternate
turns. Each turn consists of two phases: Movement and Action.

Movement Phase:

During the Movement Phase, a player can move three of their pieces
(elemental mage, guard) to an adjacent cell. Mages can move an addition
tile when they start on their favorable terrain.

Action Phase:

During the Action Phase, the player can choose one of the following
actions:

- Attack (1v1): The player selects one of their elemental mages and one
  adjacent enemy elemental mage to attack. Elemental mages can defeat
  other elemental mages of opposing elements instantly, even with a
  guard present. Guards can attack mages, but the mage will also die the
  other way around. After taking the piece of the board the attacker
  moves onto the square the defender used to stand on.

- Cast Spell: Players can use their spell tokens to cast spells,
  provided they have enough spell tokens (determined by the spell\'s
  cost). Each player can cast a maximum of two spells per turn
  (increases every 10 turns). They will be cast through the mage with
  the same element in a range of 2 in each direction (differes with the
  terrain they stand on).

Special Rules:

- Terrain Effects: Increases/Decreases range of spells by 1 if the
  wizard stands on their strong/weak terrain. Increase the amount of
  tiles he mage can move by 1 if standing on their strong terrain at the
  start of the movement phase.

- Victory Condition: The first player to eliminate all their opponent\'s
  elemental mages or the opponent\'s Spirit Mage wins the game.
