**Title: Realm of Elements**

**Overview:** \"Realm of Elements\" is a strategic two-player board game
set in a mystical world where players take on the roles of powerful
mages. Their goal is to harness the forces of nature and magic to gain
control over the realm. This game combines elements of strategy,
tactics, and spellcasting.

**Components:**

1.  **Game Board:** The game board is a square grid with 8x8 cells. Each
    cell represents a terrain type, such as Forest, Mountain, Lake, and
    Plains.

2.  **Mages:** Each player has a set of 5 mage pieces, representing
    Fire, Water, Earth, Air, and a Spirit Mage. These pieces are
    uniquely shaped.

3.  **Guards:** Each player also has a set of 5 guard pieces, which play
    a protective role in the game.

4.  **Spells:** A table of spells is available for players to cast
    during their turns. These spells are categorized into offensive,
    defensive, and utility spells.

**Setup:**

1.  Place the game board in the center of the table.

2.  Each player selects a color and places their mages and guard pieces
    on their side of the board as follows:\

3.  Spells: Both players start with 5 spell tokens and gain 1 additional
    token each turn. Every 5 turns, the token gain rate increases by 1.

**Gameplay:** \"Realm of Elements\" is played in turns, with each player
taking alternate turns consisting of two phases: Movement and Action.

**Movement Phase:** During the Movement Phase, a player can move three
of their pieces (mages or guards) to an adjacent cell. Mages receive an
extra tile of movement when starting on their favorable terrain.

**Action Phase:** During the Action Phase, the player can choose one of
the following actions:

1.  **Attack (1v1):** Choose one of your pieces to attack an adjacent
    enemy piece. Mages can eliminate opposing mages of different
    elements instantly, even if protected by a guard. Mages can attack
    guards, but both the attacker and defender are removed from the
    board. Guards can also attack mages (guards can intercept, attacker
    won't also die). The attacker takes the defender\'s place.

2.  **Cast Spell:** Spend spell tokens to cast spells, provided you have
    enough tokens (determined by the spell\'s cost). Each player can
    cast a maximum of two spells per turn (this limit increases every 10
    turns). Spells are cast by the mage of the corresponding element
    within a range of 2 in each direction. The range may vary based on
    the terrain they occupy.

3.  **Guard:** Guards can protect adjacent mages by intercepting
    attacks. If an enemy piece attempts to attack a mage protected by a
    guard, the guard is removed from the board instead of the mage.
    Additionally, spells cannot be cast through a guard (the guard
    blocks the spell).

**Special Rules:**

- **Terrain Effects:** Terrain influences movement and spell range.
  Standing on favorable terrain increases movement by 1 tile and extends
  spell range by 1 tile, while unfavorable terrain shortens spell range
  by 1 tile.

- **Victory Condition:** The first player to eliminate their opponent\'s
  Spirit Mage wins the game.
