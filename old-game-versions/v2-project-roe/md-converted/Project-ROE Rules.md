### Game idea:

Two players build up their troops to battle it out in a strategic fight,
including Guards, Champions and the King, who should be protected at all
times. Take your opponents pieces through Attacks or Skills and win the
game with your tactic, but watch out for the other player, he might just
have something up his sleeve to turn the tides in his favor.

### Goal of the game:

The winner is determined by who can take out the opponent\'s king first.
If every piece except the two kings are gone from the board it is
counted as a draw, the same goes for when no piece has been taken in 10
turns.

### Components:

1.  Game Board: The 12x12 game board is made up of 4 terrain types, each
    you can find the same amount of, which influence the movement range
    of your pieces, the Champion\'s Skill range and the armor of the
    pieces.

2.  Champions: Both players have 5 Champions on the board at the start,
    but there are in total 18 Champions of 6 classes to choose from,
    each having 2 Skills that suit their class and 1 Joker Skill from
    another class that fits the piece. Every Champion has a terrain that
    they are good on.

3.  Guards: Each player also has 6 Guards, which take up a more
    protective role for the Champions and the King in the game. They are
    neutral on every terrain.

4.  King: The King is similar to a Champion, in which he also has his 3
    Joker Skills, but he is neutral on every terrain, just like the
    Guards.

5.  Skills: Like mentioned before, all the Champions and the king have 3
    Skills which they can activate and use during their turn.

### Setup:

1.  Build the game board to your liking, with how you set up the
    terrain, but make sure that the first 6x6 corner is mirrored
    correctly, so that every area of the board is balanced and fair for
    everyone.

2.  Flip a coin for who goes first.

3.  Pick and ban phase: Player 1 starts off by banning a Champion they
    dislike and picking their first two and where they should go on the
    board, afterwards Player 2 does the same, which gets repeated once
    more for each player, then Player 1 takes his final Champion and
    Player 2 does the same. Both sides can take the same Champions, so
    Player 1's Champion of type A could fight against Player 2's
    Champion of type A, but both players can't take the same type of
    Champion twice.

4.  Each player receives 5 Skill Runes, abbreviated to Runes, at the
    start of the game.

5.  Player 1 starts the first turn.

### Gameplay:

How a single turn is played:\
(GAME NAME) is played in turns, which consist of a movement phase and an
action phase. The game starts at turn 1 and then counts up by 0.5 when a
player is done with both phases, so a full turn consists of both players
having made their respectable actions.

Movement Phase:\
During the movement phase, a player has 2 Move Slots. They can use these
slots to move up to two different pieces by 1 tile each, in any
direction. Terrain can influence these movements, but the minimum
movement range is 1. Guards, however, can move up to 2 tiles as long as
terrain or injuries don't hinder them. Players don't have to use their
Move Slots if they choose; they can move just one piece or skip the
phase entirely. Additionally, when guards move onto enemy pieces, it's
considered an attack.

Action Phase:

In the action phase a player has 2 Skill Slots at the start of the game,
which can be used to activate a Skill like the name implies.

A complete turn comes to an end:

Now that both players have finished their turn they get 1 more Rune.
Every 7 turns (7, 14, 21...) this tally increases by 1, so after and
including round 7 both players receive 2 Runes and so on. The amount of
Skill Slots in the action phase also increases from the starting point
of 2, this happens every 10 turns (10, 20, 30...), so after and
including round 10 both players can use 3 Skill Slots.

Attack System:

If you move onto a tile that holds an opponent\'s piece you will take
that piece and afterwards move to that space, if not done through a
movement skill. If the attacked piece is a Champion, but has a Guard
adjacent to it, the Guard is taken instead and you will remain in your
current position. If there are more than one Guard the closest one to
your piece gets taken and if there is still no decision the one closest
to the middle of the board gets taken.

Skill System:\
The most complex system in the game is by far the Skill system. A single
Skill has 5 different attributes that describe it: Name, Cost, Champion,
Class and Effect. When choosing to cast a Skill you first need to check
if you can afford to do so, if not you won't be able to activate it,
otherwise you are free to go. Each Champ (and the king) has 3 Skills: 2
that are of the Champs Class and 1 from another Class. The standard
Skill range is 2, except the king's who's is 3, which is affected by the
terrain the Champ stands on, going up or down by 1. Some Skills may
affect this as well. This is further explained in the champion classes
part.

Health System:

All Pieces have 2 States they can be in: Normal and Injured, so if 1 DMG
is dealt it gets injured, an additional 1 DMG hit will remove the piece
from the board. If a piece is injured, its movement is capped at 1 and
the skill range is reduced by 1. This is the damage chart:\
Attack - 2 DMG \| Offense Skill - 1 DMG \| (When the Effect is of the
damaging type) Terrain Effect - 1 DMG when: stepped on by a piece,
casted on a piece or the piece\'s turn ends whilst standing on it.
Pieces can also be healed from being injured as well as getting armor
that can stack, which prevents 1 DMG for each armor point a piece has.
Receiving armor when injured will not stop the injured effects from
affecting the piece. 3 Armor is the maximum.

Champion Classes:

Champs are of 6 different classes that change their basic use. Each
class holds 3 different champions that still vary from their specific
uses inside the class scope.

Offense - These Champs are attackers, whose main target is dealing
damage directly. Their skills are used to damage opponent pieces by 1,
unless affected by something that ups it. Offense Skills can also be
intercepted by Guards, so a Champion standing behind one can't be hit,
at least by offense skills. There are a few rules on how the Skill path
is determined:![](media/image1.png){width="3.389922353455818in"
height="3.3584372265966755in"}

You pretty much stay either diagonally or in a straight line, until you
have to branch off to reach your destination, the straight line being
preferred. The Skill path is only used for Offense Skills or when pieces
are being moved by any other skill, so they trace along a certain amount
toward a target.

The base cost of an Offense Skill is 2, which will go up by 1 when the
Champion has an extra effect on top of damaging the targeted piece.

Mobility - Mobility Champions are used to move around pieces on the
board. This has many offensive or defensive use cases. You need to
mention that you can't use this movement as an attack by moving for
example a guard onto an opponent\'s piece. Each Mobility Skill handles
full target squares differently, so there aren't default rules in place.
Mobility Skills are also given an additional range to boost usability.

Boost - The role is pretty straightforward: give effects, buffs or
similar to your pieces. This can include healing injuries, or just
adding armor.

Defense - Defensive Champions have the important role of preventing the
need of having a healer. Defense Skills have three variants:

Weak Shield - 2 Rounds - 1 DMG absorb

Strong Shield - 2 Rounds - 2 DMG absorb

Mirror Shield - 2 Rounds - Apply DMG to both Attacker/Skill User and the
Afflicted Piece

The price obviously changes with the type of shield. The one major
difference between armor and shields is that armor is permanent until
destruction and shields are either destroyed or run out. They can only
be cast on a piece in the skill range, nowhere else. Casting it on a
piece with an active shield will stack the length the shield will stay
active. It also stops offense skills from going through the piece with
the shield, which the armor does not do. Defense Skills only take up ½ a
Skill Slot in the Action Phase, so you can decide to use one twice in
space for another skill.

on. These are the different Effects that can be applied:

Damage Effect - Deals DMG to piece standing on it, precise rules in the
Health System Part

Blocked Effect - Makes the affected tile inaccessible to be landed or
stood on, also makes it unable to use Skills with paths going through it
or landing on it.

Joker - A 'jack of all trades' class, these champions don't have class
specific skills, rather taking 3 skills from any class or have ones that
don't fit the description of others.

Terrain Types and their effects on the pieces on them:

1)  Water

    a)  1 Armor is deactivated, which will make DMG ignore it.

    b)  The Skill Range is increased by 1.

2)  Forrest

    a)  1 Temporary Armor is added, which will be removed after stepping
        off the terrain. If it was destroyed nothing gets taken.

    b)  The Skill Range is lowered by 1.

3)  Plains

    a)  1 Armor is deactivated, which will make DMG ignore it.

    b)  The movement range is increased by 1.

4)  Mountains

    a)  The movement range is lowered by 1.

    b)  The Skill Range is increased by 1.

When a Champion is standing on its Good Terrain the negative Effect gets
nullified and the third aspect that wasn\'t changed gets increased.

These are the new effects when this rule is applied:

1)  Water

    a)  The movement range is increased by 1.

    b)  The Skill Range is increased by 1.

2)  Forrest

    a)  1 Temporary Armor is added, which will be removed after stepping
        off the terrain. If it was destroyed nothing gets taken.

    b)  The movement range is increased by 1.

3)  Plains

    a)  The Skill Range is increased by 1.

    b)  The movement range is increased by 1.

4)  Mountains

    a)  1 Temporary Armor is added, which will be removed after stepping
        off the terrain. If it was destroyed nothing gets taken.

    b)  The Skill Range is increased by 1.
